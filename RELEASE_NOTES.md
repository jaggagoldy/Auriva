## 🌟 What's New in Auriva 1.3

Auriva 1.3 delivers **Practice Operations Excellence (POE-001)** — upgrading both Front-Desk Reception and Clinical Doctor Workbenches for maximum speed and zero friction.

---

### 🏥 Workstream B1: Reception Excellence

* **1-Click Patient Check-in (`REQ-REC-001`)**
  Front-desk receptionists can check in arriving patients with a single click. Queue numbers are automatically assigned in sequential order per doctor per day.

* **Rapid Walk-in Registration (`REQ-REC-002`)**
  Register walk-in patients in under 30 seconds! Simply enter the patient's phone number or name — Auriva automatically matches existing patient records to eliminate duplicate entries. Emergency walk-ins can be queued instantly without required fields.

* **Emergency Patient Queue Bypass (`REQ-REC-003`)**
  Prioritize critical patients instantly. Emergency status bumps patients to position #1 in the doctor's queue with a clear red **`[ EMERGENCY ]`** badge and alerts the attending doctor.

* **Doctor Queue Transfers (`REQ-REC-004`)**
  Reassign waiting patients between doctor queues with 1 click. Queue tokens re-index automatically to maintain a clean calling sequence.

* **Instant Cashier Checkout Workspace (`REQ-REC-005`)**
  Complete billing and payment collection in seconds. Manage itemized clinical fees, apply authorized concessions, split payments across Cash/Card/UPI, generate branded printable receipts, and settle visits seamlessly.

---

### 🩺 Workstream B2: Doctor Excellence

* **Uninterrupted Consultation Workbench Charting (`REQ-DOC-001`)**
  Complete patient consultations in under 60 seconds! Record chief complaints, vitals, clinical notes, diagnosis, prescriptions, and procedure orders on a single, clean workspace without page reloads.

* **Structured Prescription Authoring & Printable Rx (`REQ-DOC-002`)**
  Author structured prescriptions effortlessly. Search from medicine catalogs, pick dosage presets, add timing instructions, and generate branded printable Rx PDFs (`RX-` numbered).

* **1-Click Consultation Sign-off & Automated Invoicing (`REQ-DOC-003`)**
  Sign off visits with 1 click. Consultation status transitions automatically, consultation fee invoices draft instantly, and printable Visit Summaries (`VS-` numbered) are generated for the patient.

* **Doctor Schedule & Date Time-Blocking Engine (`REQ-DOC-004`)**
  Manage doctor calendar availability and block out custom time slots (procedures, meetings, personal time) with buffer protection to prevent double-booking.

* **Doctor Leave & Holiday Management Engine (`REQ-DOC-005`)**
  Record planned doctor leaves and clinic holidays. Patient appointment booking slots are automatically suppressed during leave days without HRMS bloat.
