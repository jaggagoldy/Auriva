Edge cases
  • Doctor cancels / takes leave — system proposes 3 nearest alternatives (same doctor other day, or similar doctor same day); one-tap rebook.
  • Doctor running late — live queue shows revised estimate; bulk "running 20 min late" SMS from front desk (J3).
  • Patient no-show — status → No-show after grace window; gentle rebook nudge next day; repeated no-shows may require prepayment (future).
  • Booking for a family member — patient selects member context before slot PAT-016/017; records route to that member.
  • No smartphone at clinic — everything works via SMS token + front desk; app is never mandatory for care.
Alternative flows
  • Walk-in — skips discovery/booking entirely; enters at J3 walk-in registration; the account is created by reception and claimed later by the patient via OTP.
  • Rebook a known doctor — from Home or history: 2 taps PAT-001→PAT-007.
  • Lab-only journey — book test MKT-005 → sample → report ready notification → trends PAT-011/012.

Network drop mid-bookingDraft persisted server-side; resume link via SMS; flow re-opens at the same step.
Queue feed stale / offlineLast-updated timestamp always visible; pull-to-refresh; falls back to SMS updates.
Payment failure (when prepay ships)Slot held 10 min; retry or switch to pay-at-clinic without losing the slot.

  • Symptom-based triage suggesting speciality before search.
  • Insurance / cashless eligibility shown at booking.
  • ABDM-fetched history from other providers into the timeline.

Edge cases
  • Emergency jumps the queue — reception escalates (J3); board reorders; doctor sees a red Emergency card next with reason.
  • Duplicate patient record spotted — doctor flags from workbench; admin resolves via merge ORG-009; consult continues on the flagged record.
  • Two clinics in one day — Today view is per-affiliation; switching clinic context switches queue and fee rules.
  • Patient refuses digital Rx — print view exists; record still stored digitally.

Edge cases
  • Patient at the wrong branch — cross-branch booking search; transfer the booking or rebook locally with fee carried over.
  • Elderly patient without a phone — register under a family member's number with a relationship tag; records still per-person.
  • Duplicate records found at the desk — attach to the most recent, flag for admin merge ORG-009; never block check-in on data hygiene.
  • Token display screen offline — desk announces verbally; patient app remains the source of truth.
  • Doctor absent unexpectedly — one action reassigns or bulk-reschedules that doctor's day; all patients notified.
Alternative flows
  • Phone booking — receptionist books on the patient's behalf inside Appointments Hub ORG-005; patient gets SMS confirmation and can claim the account later.
  • QR self-check-in (P1) — patient scans at the desk; receptionist only handles exceptions.
  • Follow-up fast lane — pre-booked follow-ups auto-appear; check-in is a single confirm.

Edge cases
  • Doctor leaves the org — offboarding wizard: future appointments bulk-reassigned or rebooked, patients notified, access revoked same minute.
  • Role misconfiguration locks staff out — owner always retains a recovery path; permission changes take effect without re-login.
  • Branch operates differently — services, prices and booking rules override per branch; defaults inherit from org.
  • Regulatory audit request — export access logs and consent trail for a date range from SET-008.
  • Dispute over a consult record — record versions + signature trail are immutable and reviewable.
Alternative flows
  • Chain onboarding — bulk branch + doctor import (future); until then, branch-by-branch with cloned settings.
  • Delegated administration — branch managers get admin scoped to one branch; owner keeps org-wide view.
  • Solo-practice upgrade — an independent doctor's lightweight practice (J5) converts into a full org without data migration.
