AUTH-001Sign in / Sign up

One phone number. No "create account" toggle.

The gap you flagged — there's no sign-up today, only a role-select login for seeded demo users. This screen replaces the role-select gateway: everyone starts here, existing or new, patient or doctor. The system tells them apart after OTP — nobody has to declare it upfront.

AUTH-002OTP Verification

Verify, then resolve who they are.

After this screen, the backend looks up the phone number. Known + patient → straight to Home. Known + doctor → straight to Today's Queue. Unknown number → branches to onboarding (next two sections). No manual role picker anymore — defaultWorkspacePathForRole already does this server-side resolution, it just needs a real identity to resolve.

AUTH-004Patient Onboarding

Minimal profile. Under a minute to Home.

Only shown to a phone number the system has never seen. Three required fields, one optional ABHA link that can be completed later from Profile — the goal is speed, not completeness. Everything else (address, insurance, family) is filled in later from a friendlier, lower-stakes moment: the Profile screen.

PRO-001Patient App · Profile

This is the answer to "what does a user want to see about themselves."

Not just an edit form. Three things patients actually want here: their own details (identity, health ID, medical basics), their care network made visible (which doctors, which clinic, which pharmacy, which lab — the breadth of the platform, shown as real cards not a settings list), and control (insurance, consent, security). This is the direct answer to "the more detail it looks, the more interactive."
