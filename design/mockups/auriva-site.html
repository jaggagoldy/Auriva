Questions

Everything you're wondering.

Is Auriva for me if I'm a solo practitioner?
Yes — Auriva Solo Practice is live today and free forever. You get the full platform, but only ever see the part you need. Setup takes about two minutes.
What makes it a "platform" and not just clinic software?
Everything connects. A prescription you write flows to labs, pharmacies and insurers on the same system — no faxes, no re-typing. As new editions launch (hospitals, labs, pharmacy), they plug into the same core you already use.
What can patients do on Auriva?
Find and book doctors, get WhatsApp reminders, pay by UPI, and keep every prescription, record and lab report for life — for themselves and their family. Always free.
What happens when I grow into a clinic or hospital?
Nothing breaks. The same login and data simply unlock more — a shared calendar, more doctors, departments, admissions. You never migrate to different software.
Is it built for India?
Deeply. UPI payments, WhatsApp reminders, rupee billing and cashless insurance rails — shaped with practitioners here, not translated after the fact.
Who owns the data?
You do — provider and patient both. Export anytime, zero lock-in, and a tamper-evident log of every change.

Questions

Pricing, answered.

Is Solo Practice really free?
Yes — free forever for one practitioner, with the complete product and no feature locks. No card, no trial clock. You only pay when you add a team.
Do I pay per patient or per appointment?
Never. Unlimited patients and appointments are included. There are no usage meters and no per-feature upsells.
What does the ₹999/mo plan add?
Everything in Solo Practice, plus a team — additional doctors and reception staff, a shared clinic calendar and queue, and practice insights. It kicks in only when you grow beyond a solo practice.
What happens when I add a second doctor?
You move onto the team plan on the same account — same login, same patients, same history. Nothing to export, nothing to re-learn.
Can I leave and take my data?
Anytime. Export your records whenever you like — zero lock-in. It's your practice and your patients' data.
Is Auriva free for patients too?
Always. Booking, reminders, records, prescriptions and family health are free for patients, forever.

Popular questions
How do I set up my clinic on Auriva?
Create your account, add your services and hours, and share your booking page. Most practitioners are taking bookings within a couple of minutes.
Can patients book without an app?
Yes. Patients book from your public profile link in any browser and get a WhatsApp confirmation — no download required.
How do walk-ins work with the queue?
Add a walk-in and they join your live queue alongside booked patients, so you always know who's next.
How do I collect payment?
Create a line-item invoice at the end of a visit and collect by UPI or cash. The receipt is instant and the record updates automatically.
Can I export my data?
Anytime — your patients and records are yours, with zero lock-in. Reach out and we'll help you export.
