What this is

One workspace, one person, one calm day

A solo clinic has no separate front desk or doctor console — the single practitioner does everything from /clinic. Every screen answers exactly one question and always offers one obvious next action. The defining loop is Today → Consultation → Payment. Signs in with phone + password as a super_admin owner and lands here.

◎

All-in-one

no role-switching

Reception, doctor and billing capabilities are one account. /doctor & /staff are dormant until multi-clinic.

◷

Action-first

next action, not menus

The hero always shows the single most important thing to do next — see a patient, collect a payment, finish setup.

✽

Honest data

no fake UI

Every number and record is real or an honest empty state. Vitals are captured live, never fabricated.

Left sidebar on desktop, bottom tab bar on mobile. A demo clinic shows a reset banner; a solo owner sees a warm honey/pine workspace.

The defining loop

Consultation journey — arrival to paid

Started from Today’s Start consultation. A full-screen documentation workbench, then a signed summary, then payment. The booking status advances at each step and is reflected everywhere.

Booked status: scheduled

Patient booked online, by phone-in, or via the in-clinic Book a patient dialog.

Start consultation → in_consultation

Opens the full-width workbench with the patient’s real clinical context on the left.

Document the visit

Chief complaint, examination, diagnosis, prescription, recommended tests, follow-up, treatment & fee.

Review & complete → completed

A signed, printable visit summary. Completing auto-drafts the invoice.

Collect payment Payment due → Paid

Record cash / UPI / card (or skip). Today flips the patient to Seen · Paid.

Surfaces everywhere

The completed visit + prescription + recommended tests appear on the patient’s portal timeline and Health Vault.

Workbench anatomy

Left clinical rail real data

  • Vitals — live capture (BP, pulse, temp, SpO₂); folded into the note. No fabricated readings.
  • Allergies + chronic conditions
  • Current medicines (from the last visit)
  • Past visits (recent completed)

Main documentation column

  • Templates bar — apply a saved SOAP note (P6)
  • Chief complaint + quick suggestion chips
  • Examination & notes
  • Diagnosis — chips
  • Prescription builder — medicine · dosage · frequency · duration, with one-tap templates
  • Investigations — structured diagnostics selector (P5), recommends tests to the patient
  • Follow-up & advice — review-in segments + advice
  • Treatment & fee — the visit charge
Lifecycle, honestly. Booked → Checked-in (implicit for solo) → In consultation → Consultation completed → Payment pending → Paid → Visit completed. The Today schedule refreshes on completion so a just-seen patient never lingers as “scheduled”.

P5 · replaces the removed Labs

Diagnostics — recommend, don’t manage Live

Auriva does not own or run labs. The doctor recommends tests; the patient acts on them. In the consult, “Investigations” is a structured selector; the tests land in the patient’s Health Vault.

🔬

Doctor — selector

  • Search with acronyms (CBC, USG, MRI, HbA1c…)
  • 6 categories: Pathology · Biochemistry · Endocrinology · Microbiology · Radiology · Cardiology
  • One-click packages: Fever · Metabolic · Antenatal · Thyroid
  • 30-test catalog with prep notes
🧾

Patient — Health Vault

  • “Recommended by Dr X · date”
  • Preparation instructions (fasting / full bladder / no metal)
  • Status tracker: Pending → Booked → Completed → Report uploaded
  • Upload their own report
P6

Clinical templates Live

Reusable SOAP notes so doctors stop retyping. In the consult, a Templates bar applies a saved note; “Save current” turns the open visit into a template; “Manage” edits, favourites and deletes.

Subjective → chief complaintObjective → examinationAssessment → diagnosisPlan · Advice · Exercises → adviceFollow-up (days)Favourite ★

Applying fills empty consult fields and appends to non-empty ones — nothing is lost. Stored per doctor within the clinic (share-across-clinic is a future flag).
