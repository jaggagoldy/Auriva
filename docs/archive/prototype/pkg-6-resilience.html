04

Error states — three tiers

Every error: icon + plain explanation + recovery action. Never a stack trace, code, or bare “Something went wrong.”

Recoverable

Couldn't load appointments

Check your connection and try again.

Action failed

Payment couldn't be recorded

No money was moved. Please try again.

Critical

Clinic unavailable

We can't reach this clinic right now.

4b

Success states — close the loop

The completion of the lifecycle. Confirm what happened, offer an optional next step, and never interrupt the flow.

Patient

You're booked

Dr. Iyer · Today 4:30 PM.

Reception

₹500 collected

UPI · receipt ready.

Doctor

Prescription sent

Shared to the patient's records.

Owner

Invitation accepted

Priya joined as Nurse.

Rule: most successes are a toast (transient); a few earn a screen (booking, checkout). Never a blocking modal for a good outcome.

Capabilities are absent, not greyed. When reached by URL, explain in plain language and offer a path (SAD-043 §7, APS-045 X1).

06

Notifications — when to use which

Toast (transient confirm) · Banner (persistent context) · Inline (validation) · Modal (blocking decision).

Toast

Transient confirmation. Auto-dismiss ~2.5s.

Banner

Persistent context until resolved.

Your plan expires in 5 days.

Inline

Field / section validation.

Enter a valid 10-digit mobile number.

Modal

Blocking decision / destructive confirm.

08

Dark-mode audit

Not an inversion — audited via tokens. Toggle the theme (top bar) and everything on this page re-tunes.

Contrast ≥ 4.5:1 text, ≥ 3:1 UI, both themes
Semantic status colours constant — a red/amber that shifts at night is a clinical-safety risk
Queue aging (yellow/orange/red) legible on dark
Hero / night gradients hold; disabled reads as disabled
Toasts, focus rings, chips tokened — not hard-coded
09

Accessibility baseline · WCAG 2.1 AA

The platform standard for every screen.

Keyboard — every action reachable; Enter confirms, Esc cancels; ⌘K palette (staff)
Visible focus — 2.5px ring, never removed
Reduced motion — no shimmer/slide; instant state changes
Touch targets ≥ 44×44px for primary controls
Screen-reader labels — icon-only buttons carry aria-label; aria-live for toasts/queue
Colour independence — status paired with icon/label, never colour alone
10
