# Release 1.4 Strategic Roadmap & Discovery Outline

> **Milestone Target:** Release 1.4  
> **Status:** 🟡 PLANNING & DISCOVERY PHASE  
> **Predecessor Milestone:** Release 1.3 (POE-001) — 🔒 CLOSED & IMMUTABLE

---

## 1. RELEASE 1.4 OBJECTIVES

With Milestone POE-001 (Practice Operations Excellence) successfully closed and live in production, Release 1.4 shifts focus to **Advanced Practice Intelligence, Digital Health Integrations, and Expanded Patient Engagement**.

---

## 2. CANDIDATE FEATURE EPICS

1. **Epic 1: Clinical Teleconsultation & Video Health Hub**
   - WebRTC video consultation workspace for remote doctor-patient visits.
   - Integrated digital prescription generation during video calls.

2. **Epic 2: Advanced Analytics & Financial Forecasting**
   - Predictive revenue modeling, doctor capacity utilization, and seasonal patient visit trends.
   - Exportable Excel / CSV practice accounting ledgers.

3. **Epic 3: Patient Portal Experience & Self-Scheduling**
   - Patient self-service slot booking with online payment gateway integration (Razorpay / UPI).
   - Digital intake forms and pre-consultation medical history submission.

4. **Epic 4: Inventory & Pharmacy Integration**
   - Real-time clinic pharmacy stock tracking, low-inventory alerts, and auto-dispensing log on prescription sign-off.
