# Release 1.4 Product Office Epic Prioritization Matrix

> **Milestone Target:** Release 1.4  
> **Status:** 🟡 DISCOVERY & PRIORITIZATION PHASE  
> **Purpose:** Evaluation and scoring matrix to select high-impact epics for Release 1.4 BRD inclusion.

---

## 1. EPIC EVALUATION MATRIX

Each epic is scored from 1 (Lowest) to 5 (Highest) across 6 commercial and operational criteria:

| Epic Name | Customer Value (1-5) | Revenue Potential (1-5) | Implementation Effort (1-5) | Strategic Differentiation (1-5) | Dependencies (1-5) | GTM Impact (1-5) | Weighted Score | Prioritization Rank |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Epic 1: Patient Portal & Self-Scheduling** | 5 | 5 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 5 | **27 / 30** | 🥇 **RANK 1 (RECOMMENDED)** |
| **Epic 2: Clinical Teleconsultation & Video Hub**| 5 | 4 | 2 | 5 | 4 | 4 | **24 / 30** | 🥈 **RANK 2 (HIGH PRIORITY)** |
| **Epic 3: Advanced Analytics & Forecasting** | 4 | 4 | 3 | 3 | 5 | 3 | **22 / 30** | 🥉 **RANK 3** |
| **Epic 4: Inventory & Pharmacy Integration** | 3 | 3 | 2 | 3 | 3 | 3 | **17 / 30** | 4️⃣ **RANK 4 (DEFERRED)** |

---

## 2. PRODUCT OFFICE RECOMMENDATIONS

1. **Primary Focus for Release 1.4 (Rank 1 & Rank 2):**
   - **Patient Portal & Self-Scheduling:** Highest commercial GTM impact — enables online patient booking with instant payment collection (Razorpay/UPI) and digital intake forms.
   - **Clinical Teleconsultation & Video Hub:** High strategic differentiation — enables remote video visits and digital prescription dispensing.
2. **Deferred to Release 1.5:**
   - *Inventory & Pharmacy Integration* and *Advanced Analytics* are deferred to Release 1.5 to maintain a sharp, execution-ready scope for Release 1.4.
